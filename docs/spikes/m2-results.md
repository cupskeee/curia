## What the results change (design consequences)

1. **The recipe works; the plain SDL window does not** (owner observations). The wrapped non-activating panel under the Accessory policy was visible over CK3's Fullscreen Space at all 20 level and flag combinations; the plain always-on-top SDL window was not, even with the Accessory policy (E2). All 20 combinations include `canJoinAllSpaces` or `canJoinAllApplications`, and the lowest level tried was floating (3), so **Apple's DTS statement that floating and status levels sit below fullscreen content does not hold for this panel in this setup** (research-notes C34). Which element of the recipe is *necessary* (panel class, policy, flags, level) was not isolated. The real app starts from combination 1 (the documented recipe) and M4 may narrow it.
2. **Placement follows CK3's display, on every show.** The probe placed the overlay once, on the display under the pointer, and kept it there. The real app must place it on the display that contains CK3's window each time it is shown, using the rectangle from `CGWindowListCopyWindowInfo` for the `ck3` process (readable without Screen Recording, E9), and fall back to the pointer's display only if that fails (new FR-OVL-9).
3. **The in-game button and the hot key are the primary controls; the tray is secondary** (owner decision, 2026-10-05). On CK3's Fullscreen display the menu bar did not appear (two-display setup), so the tray cannot be relied on during play. The in-game button (T7) and the Carbon hot key both worked.
4. **Do not rely on `ignoresMouseEvents` for click-through under SDL.** It went back to 0 within seconds in both periods; the SDL source (3.4.18) resets it on mouse-motion events for transparent windows, a source reading not confirmed at runtime. The plan stays a window sized to the chat panel (no transparent hit area); per-pixel pass-through, if ever needed, goes through `SDL_SetWindowShape`, which was not tested.
5. **Tray creation activates the app** (SDL calls `activateIgnoringOtherApps` unconditionally; the log shows the activation in the same millisecond). The real app creates the tray at start-up, before CK3 is frontmost, never while the player is in the game, and may use a native status item instead if that proves necessary.
6. **Focus behaviour is acceptable:** the first click on the overlay made the app report itself active and the panel key, and it received the keys, while NSWorkspace still reported CK3 frontmost and CK3 stayed in fullscreen; Esc returns the keys to CK3 immediately. The two sources disagree while the panel is key and the log cannot say which governs key routing; the real app takes "is CK3 in front" from the NSWorkspace frontmost application (a design choice; CK3 is identified by the executable name `ck3`, its bundle id is empty).
7. **Start-up policy:** every launch logged the Regular policy right after `SDL_Init` and then the explicit switch to Accessory, including the stages that set `SDL_HINT_MAC_BACKGROUND_APP` first, so the hint alone did not give Accessory and was not tested alone. The real Info.plist sets `LSUIElement` and the app also sets the Accessory policy explicitly; M4 checks that no Dock icon shows.
8. **Permissions:** the real app never calls an Accessibility API (the probe's own `AXIsProcessTrusted()` made the system log an Accessibility request and probably created the stale row) and never plays sound through `NSSound` (microphone check). Research-notes §2.5.
9. **T7 passes in Fullscreen:** a mod-added HUD widget works through `gui/scripted_widgets/` without overriding a vanilla file; the click effect ran with a constant character (consistent with the player, owner confirmation pending); `[GetCurrentDate.GetString]` resolves inside `debug_log`. The log file's modification time matched the last click line's second in both helper runs (about 1 s resolution, helper run 4 to 7 s later): a first data point for the flush question, not the M3 measurement (T3).

## Problems found in the probe (fixed before the session ran)

- **Crash on app-activation notifications:** a notification block used a pointer to a dead local; the probe died on the first such notification (both E1 attempts at 19:09 and the earlier tray self-test). Fixed; covered by a self-test that needs no window (`probe_observers`, also run by ctest and in CI) and by `tools/m2/preflight.sh`.
- **Accessibility and microphone requests:** removed (design consequence 8).
- **Probe layout:** the second E4 text line is clipped; cause not established (see the E4 row); not fixed, the probe is throwaway.

## Open items

- **E6 with CK3 frontmost:** typing in the overlay while CK3 is the frontmost app, and whether Space, the digits 1–5 or letters reach CK3. The session's typing phase had another app in front. A short re-run of the `interactive` stage (click the overlay while CK3 is in front, type, watch the pause state and game speed) closes it; otherwise it moves to the M4 testing.
- **Dock icon:** reported in the tray runs (the logs hold two complete Terminal-in-front runs, one CK3-in-front run and one aborted launch; the owner's notes say "both tray runs"), not explained: the policy is `accessory` in every logged snapshot, including right before and after tray creation. A short controlled run afterwards (output not kept) read the LaunchServices application type as `UIElement` before, during and after tray creation, and the Dock's recent-apps list held no entry for the probe. What exactly was seen (a normal tile with a running dot, or a recent-apps tile; whether it disappeared when the probe quit; whether the other stages also showed it) is not known.
- **Click-through that holds:** whole-window click-through did not hold for the nominal 10 s (design consequence 4); per-pixel pass-through is untested.
- **Which recipe element is necessary:** not isolated (a plain panel without the non-activating style, no collection behaviour, a level below 3).
- **Windowed (E10, T7 in Windowed):** deferred to the M4 testing.
- **T7 character:** the owner confirms that the logged character is their ruler (the click lines show one constant character).
- **Rung 3 (a normal companion window):** never exercised; it is the expected fallback, untested.
- **Not covered:** other macOS versions, an Intel Mac, a signed or notarised bundle, a single-display setup, and CK3 at other resolutions; Windows and Linux are CI plus testers.
